# 26 November closeout: character keepsakes

**Classification:** shared
**Roadmap day:** Day 19

## Outcomes delivered

1. Character JSON may hold optional party-visible letters, sketches, handouts
   and mementos with localized copy, image alternatives and stable evidence.
2. The lazy Character Journal adds a dedicated collection without lengthening
   the profile or loading its images before the reader opens it.
3. Every image opens in the established accessible lightbox; a focused print
   action isolates page-break-safe keepsake cards.
4. Campaign Manager can add, replace, link and remove keepsakes manually and
   rejects unknown evidence, duplicate IDs, missing alt text and private
   notebook sources.
5. The AI contract is provider-neutral and review-first. Automatic proposals
   remain explicitly unavailable until they use the same validator.

## Cross-cutting analysis

- Keepsakes reuse the published story graph and never become a second source of
  narrative truth.
- The active Quest Tracker work may link a quest update to a keepsake later,
  but neither record owns or copies the other.
- Day 20 visual moods can style cards and print paper without changing the
  evidence, locale or privacy contract delivered here.
- Completed campaigns without `keepsakes` remain valid; Phandelver and Curse of
  Strahd exercise this empty path while the original public demo exercises the
  populated path.
- Catalog growth measures 512,300 public and 560,437 production initial bytes.
  Only the aggregate ceilings move, to 513,000 and 561,000 bytes; request,
  largest-asset, deferred-script and image ceilings remain fixed.

## Evidence boundary

The public template adds one original SVG letter to Mira Thorn's fictional demo
archive. Production campaign canon and protected media are unchanged. No
private notes, transcripts, credentials, licensed compendium text or protected
art crossed repositories.
