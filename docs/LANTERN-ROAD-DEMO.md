# The Lantern Road demo journey

**Roadmap:** Day 07 — richer Lantern Road player journey  
**Audience:** first-time and returning players  
**Goals:** GN-01, GN-02, GN-03 and GN-07

The Lantern Road is QuestKeeper's original, redistributable campaign fixture.
It demonstrates the real player experience without exposing a private table,
licensed adventure text or a product tour made only of empty placeholders.

## The two-minute path

1. Enter **The Lantern Road** through the normal spoiler-consent boundary.
2. Read the campaign brief to learn where the Wayfarers are and what changed.
3. Open **The Shadow of the Beacon** for the latest evidence.
4. Follow Aria's character thread or inspect the Glass Beacon location.
5. Return through the remembered trail or prepare the public offline pack.

The path is guidance, not a locked tutorial. Every destination is an ordinary
page, remains keyboard reachable and can be explored in another order.

## Stable fixture facts

The public validation fixture intentionally makes these answers discoverable:

- **Current place:** inside the ruined Glass Beacon.
- **Latest change:** its cold flame reacted to Aria's hidden lens shard.
- **Immediate choice:** follow the northbound shadow before more lanterns fail.
- **Aria's concern:** the impossible light knows her name, and she delays asking
  for help.
- **Mira's concern:** the century-wet letter is written in her own hand.
- **Open connection:** Sable Finch returned alone from the eastern pass and
  refuses to finish its map.

The demo does not reveal protected profile fields on the public campaign home.
Character pages still demonstrate the explicit password boundary separately.

## Content depth contract

The fixture keeps at least two heroes, two known NPCs, two locations and two
completed chapters. Every hero has a public diary, per-session knowledge and a
memorable quote; every chapter has a location, at least three narrative beats
and at least three key events. English and Italian remain complete together.

Run `npm run test:demo` to guard this contract. The test also catches likely
text-encoding corruption and verifies that the campaign's central open threads
remain searchable.

## Rights and privacy

All Lantern Road names, prose and vector artwork are original to QuestKeeper.
The fixture contains no private transcript, real player identity, credential,
licensed campaign canon or rulebook text. Future demo enrichment must preserve
that boundary even when a production campaign inspires a reusable feature.
