The Companion is the **player-facing campaign website**. It turns scattered
notes, sheets and images into a place the party can understand before a session
and revisit after it.

Try the [live sample campaign](https://mtamagno.github.io/questkeeper-rpg-campaign-manager/)
without installing anything.

## Enter a chronicle

The home page lists every campaign in the archive. Choose one to see its title,
status and premise. A spoiler warning appears before entry; accepting it is
remembered on that device so it does not interrupt every visit.

The campaign language selector follows the languages configured by the GM. The
Manager interface language and campaign content languages are independent.

## Find what matters

Every campaign can include:

- **Overview:** premise, current state, latest memories and party entry points.
- **Party:** public character profiles, portraits, biography, personality,
  hobbies, quick facts, meaningful objects, favourite moments and personal lore
  chapters.
- **Private sheet:** password-protected statistics, equipment, currency,
  features and prepared spells, including links to descriptions where supplied.
- **Progression:** previous level snapshots and a fantasy-styled PDF export.
- **Chronicle:** session summaries and longer illustrated prose chapters.
- **People & bonds:** NPC records and relationship maps for party and world.
- **Atlas:** maps, discovered locations and campaign artwork.
- **Personal notebooks:** optional protected character journals with page-like
  navigation rather than an endless feed.

Content appears only when the GM has added it; a new campaign can start small
and grow after every session.

## Public and private information

Public profiles are intended for the table. Password-protected sections can
hold personal backstory, secrets or a full sheet, but they are **lightweight
table privacy**, not a substitute for secure hosting. Anyone with the static
site files may be able to inspect their contents.

Never place real-world secrets, API keys, account passwords or personal data in
campaign content.

## Before game night

A useful two-minute refresher is:

1. Read the latest session recap.
2. Check your character's current sheet and prepared options.
3. Review party bonds and unresolved NPCs.
4. Open the relevant map or location.

QuestKeeper is designed to return players to the table, not keep them browsing
the archive all evening.

## On phones and tablets

The Companion is responsive and works in modern browsers. Portrait phones keep
navigation and reading compact; wider screens give artwork, relationships and
character sheets more room.

On a supported browser, use **Install** in the top command bar to give
QuestKeeper its own launcher icon and standalone window. On iPhone and iPad,
use **Share → Add to Home Screen** instead. The
[PWA installation guide](PWA-INSTALLATION.md) explains the current offline
boundary as well as both install paths. Before travelling, use the campaign's
**Offline pack** to choose public characters and save the three newest recaps;
follow the [game-night checklist](OFFLINE-GAME-NIGHT.md) to verify the exact
device-local copy.

Native Android and iOS editions are part of the
[public roadmap](../ROADMAP.md).
