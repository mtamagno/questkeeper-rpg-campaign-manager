# QuestKeeper Community Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make the QuestKeeper community welcoming, safe and equitable for
everyone.

We are committed to an environment that respects the dignity, rights and
contributions of every participant, regardless of race, ethnicity, caste,
colour, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or
gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language,
philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socio-economic position,
level of education or other status.

The same opportunity to participate is extended to everyone who acts in good
faith and follows this Code of Conduct.

## Encouraged behaviour

People may interpret words and actions differently because of culture,
background or native language. With that in mind, we ask everyone to:

1. Respect the purpose of the project and its community spaces.
2. Engage kindly and honestly with other people.
3. Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
4. Take responsibility for their actions and contributions.
5. Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
6. Work to repair harm when it occurs.
7. Support the well-being of the community.

## Restricted behaviour

The following behaviour, including threats or encouragement of it, is not
accepted:

1. **Harassment:** ignoring clearly expressed boundaries or continuing
   unwanted personal attention after being asked to stop.
2. **Personal attacks:** insulting, demeaning or disparaging a person or group.
3. **Stereotyping or discrimination:** judging someone's personality or
   behaviour from an identity or immutable trait.
4. **Sexualisation:** conduct that is inappropriately intimate for the context
   of this community.
5. **Breaches of confidentiality:** sharing or using private information
   without permission.
6. **Endangerment:** causing, encouraging or threatening violence or other
   harm.
7. **Misleading identity:** impersonating someone or evading an enforcement
   action through another identity.
8. **Failure to credit sources:** contributing material without the required
   attribution or permission.
9. **Unwelcome promotion:** posting commercial or marketing material outside
   the norms and purpose of the project.
10. Any other conduct that threatens the safety or well-being of the community.

## Reporting an issue

Not every disagreement is a Code of Conduct violation. When a possible
violation does occur, report it promptly and do not publish sensitive details
in a public issue.

For conduct in an issue, pull request, discussion or comment:

1. Open the item's `...` menu and choose **Report content**.
2. Choose **Report to repository admins** when GitHub offers that option.
3. If that option is unavailable, or the report concerns a repository
   maintainer, choose **Report abuse to GitHub Support** and follow GitHub's
   [reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers will prioritise the
safety, privacy and confidentiality of the people involved. Retaliation against
someone who makes a good-faith report is itself a violation of this Code of
Conduct.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct reports. Please follow the private
process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.

## Addressing and repairing harm

If the maintainers determine that this Code of Conduct has been violated, they
may use the following enforcement ladder. Steps may be skipped when the impact
or severity of an incident requires it.

1. **Warning:** a private written warning, clarification of expectations and,
   where appropriate, an acknowledgement or apology.
2. **Temporarily limited activity:** a time-limited restriction on particular
   channels or interactions.
3. **Temporary suspension:** removal from community spaces for a defined period,
   with clear conditions for returning.
4. **Permanent ban:** removal from all project community spaces when repeated or
   severe violations leave no safe alternative.

This ladder is a guideline. Maintainers may use their judgement to protect the
community, act proportionately and support meaningful repair where possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions,
commits and other official QuestKeeper community spaces. It also applies when a
person officially represents the project in public, online or offline.
